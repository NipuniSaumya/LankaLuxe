Travel tips for a smoother Sri Lanka journey

From local etiquette to transport planning, these practical notes help you travel with confidence and comfort.

🚗

Getting around

Train journeys are scenic and efficient, while private drivers are easiest for remote hills and beaches.

🧳

Pack smart

Light layers, sun protection and comfortable shoes work best for tropical days and cooler evenings.

🤝

Local etiquette

Dress modestly at temples, greet respectfully and carry small cash for villages and roadside cafés.

🌦

Best seasons

December to April is ideal for beaches; shoulder seasons bring clear skies for highland and cultural routes.
